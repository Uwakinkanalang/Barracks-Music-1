Quick command guide

Easy to use. Ready when you are.

Here are a few documented commands and shortcuts. Explore the bot's slash-command menus in Discord for more.

/play <song>Play a YouTube video or search for a song
/joinConnect to your voice channel
/nowplayingShow the current track
/queueView the music queue
/musicOpen additional music controls

Command availability depends on the bot version, permissions, and server configuration.
